FAQ

How do I keep ants out of cat food?

Use an ant-resistant bowl, wipe crumbs daily, seal dry food, and remove wet food after meals.

Can I spray ant killer near cat food?

Avoid pesticides near bowls. Treat entry points separately and follow product safety directions.

Do ant-proof cat bowls work?

They can help when paired with cleaner storage and a less messy feeding station.
