Health notes

FAQ

Why does my cat splash water everywhere?

Some cats play with water, dislike bowl placement, or prefer moving water. Start with a stable mat and heavier bowl.

Are cat fountains worth it?

They can help cats that prefer moving water, but they require regular cleaning.

Where should I put the cat water bowl?

Try a quiet spot away from litter and sometimes away from food, depending on your cat's preference.
