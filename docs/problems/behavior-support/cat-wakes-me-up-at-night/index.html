What to skip

  • Feeding from bed after the cat yells.
  • Random automatic toys that startle everyone at night.
  • Punishment. It can increase stress and attention-seeking.

Health notes

Sudden night waking, appetite changes, confusion, vocalizing, or restlessness can be medical, especially in older cats. Ask your vet if the behavior is new or intense.

FAQ

How do I stop my cat waking me up for food?

Move the food reward to a timed feeder and avoid feeding directly after wakeup demands.

Does evening play help cats sleep?

It can help, especially when followed by a small meal or puzzle feeder.

Should I ignore a cat meowing at night?

Consistency matters, but sudden or distressed vocalizing should be checked with a vet.
