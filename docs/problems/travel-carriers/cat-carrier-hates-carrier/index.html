What to skip

  • Forcing a panicked cat through a tiny front door.
  • Loose car travel outside a secured carrier.

Stress notes

If your cat is extremely fearful, ask your vet about pre-visit planning. Stop practice sessions before panic, not after.

FAQ

What carrier is best for cats that hate carriers?

A sturdy top-load or wide-opening carrier is often easier than a narrow front-only carrier.

Should I leave the carrier out?

Yes. Leaving it out with a soft pad and treats helps it become less scary over time.

How do I get a scared cat into a carrier?

Practice before trip day, use treats, keep sessions short, and ask your vet for advice if your cat panics badly.
