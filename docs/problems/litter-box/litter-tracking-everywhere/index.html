Quick verdict

For cat litter tracking everywhere, start with the box exit path: a large textured mat where the cat actually steps, a lower-tracking litter if your current litter is dusty or tiny-grained, and a small daily vacuum routine. The goal is to catch litter before it reaches soft surfaces.

What to skip

  • Buying three small mats instead of one mat placed on the real exit path.
  • Switching litter abruptly and risking box refusal.
  • Covered boxes if the cat dislikes them or they trap smell.

When gear is not enough

If your cat suddenly avoids the box, strains, cries, or urinates outside the box, call your vet. Tracking is a gear problem; painful box avoidance may not be.

FAQ

What stops cat litter tracking the most?

A large mat on the real box exit path usually helps most, especially when paired with a lower-tracking litter and quick daily vacuuming.

Why is litter stuck in my cat's paws?

Fine, damp, or dusty litter can cling to paws. Try gradual litter changes and keep the area around the box dry.

Are covered litter boxes good for tracking?

They can reduce kicking, but some cats dislike them and smell can build up. Do not force a covered box if your cat avoids it.
