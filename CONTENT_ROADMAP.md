# Cat Problem Solver Content Roadmap

Working positioning:

> Practical fixes for annoying cat problems, with what to buy first and what to skip.

Core promise:

> Solve the cat problem in front of you before buying a pile of random pet products.

This file is the editorial backlog for the static site. When creating a new page, pick a planned page from here, create the HTML source page, then update its status.

Status values:

- `published`: page already exists on the site.
- `next`: strong candidate for the next build.
- `planned`: good future page.
- `hold`: useful idea, but not first priority.

Page formula:

- Start with the cat problem, not the product.
- Include mess level, training effort, cleanup frequency, cat tolerance, and buy priority.
- Recommend what to try first, 4-8 useful product types, what to skip, and when gear is not enough.
- Use Amazon affiliate links with the Associates tag already in the URL.
- Do not manually copy Amazon prices, star ratings, review counts, Prime status, or availability.

## Site Categories

Public category structure:

- `/problems/litter-box/` - litter tracking, smell, dust, box setup, mats, scoops, liners, and small-home litter routines.
- `/problems/scratching/` - couch scratching, carpet scratching, scratcher placement, nail care, and furniture protection.
- `/problems/hair-cleaning/` - cat hair on clothes, furniture, bedding, car seats, laundry, and odor cleanup.
- `/problems/feeding-water/` - water bowl spills, ants in cat food, messy wet food, fast eating, mats, and fountains.
- `/problems/travel-carriers/` - carrier avoidance, vet trips, car cleanup, and travel staging.
- `/problems/small-home/` - vertical territory, litter placement, window perches, toy storage, and tight-room setups.
- `/problems/behavior-support/` - boredom, night waking, counter surfing, food stealing, hiding, and stress-support gear.
- `/guides/` - trust pages about what to buy first, what to skip, and when products are not enough.

The site uses a tiny static generator:

- Source HTML lives in `/src/pages/` and `/src/problems/`.
- Shared CSS and JS live in `/assets/`.
- The build output lives in `/docs/`.
- GitHub Pages should publish `/docs/`.
- Category pages and `sitemap.xml` are generated automatically.

## Content Selection Rules

When choosing the next page, prefer topics that meet at least four criteria:

- Solves a concrete cat-owner pain, not a vague product category.
- Has clear long-tail search phrasing.
- Can recommend 4-8 Amazon product types naturally.
- Has a "what to try first" and "what to skip" section.
- Can be answered on Reddit as plain advice without leading with a link.
- Includes a safety or welfare boundary where needed.
- Can internally link to at least two related problem categories.

Avoid pages that are just "best cat products" lists. The page should help a person decide what to do about a mess, habit, smell, cleaning issue, or stressful recurring moment.

## Promotion Notes

For every strong page, create:

- 1 Reddit-style helpful answer that can be posted manually with disclosure if linking.
- 3 Pinterest pin titles.
- 1 short meta description.
- 3 internal links to related pages.

Promotion angles:

- "Buy this first" beats "ultimate cat product list".
- "What to skip" builds trust.
- "Gear is not a vet" keeps the advice responsible.
- Messy, specific problems are easier to share than generic pet shopping content.
