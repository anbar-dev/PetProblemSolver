What to skip

  • Punishment, yelling, or spraying water.
  • A scratcher hidden across the room from the couch.
  • Only buying tape without adding an acceptable scratching target.

Behavior notes

Scratching marks territory, stretches muscles, and maintains claws. The fix works better when the replacement scratcher is stable, close, and rewarding.

FAQ

How do I stop my cat scratching the couch?

Protect the scratched spot and place a better scratcher beside it. Reward use of the scratcher and keep nails trimmed.

Do couch protectors work for cats?

They can protect the target surface, but they work best with a nearby scratcher your cat actually likes.

Should I declaw a cat for couch scratching?

No. Use scratcher placement, protection, nail care, and behavior help instead.
