About

Cat problem advice that starts with the problem.

Cat Problem Solver is built for people who are tired of generic pet lists and need a practical setup for one annoying situation.

What this site does

Each page starts with a real cat-owner problem: litter tracking, box smell, couch scratching, water bowl mess, carrier avoidance, or hair cleanup. The recommendation is organized around what to try first, what to buy only if it fits, and what to skip.

Pages may include Amazon affiliate links. Product copy avoids prices, ratings, review counts, and availability claims because those change too often.

What this site does not do

This site is not veterinary advice and does not diagnose behavior or health issues. Sudden litter box changes, pain signs, repeated vomiting, appetite changes, blood, straining, severe aggression, or distress are reasons to contact a vet promptly.

Gear can support a better routine, but it should not replace care, patience, gradual introductions, or professional help when the problem needs it.
